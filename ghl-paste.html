Before you come in

What happens next

Three steps, and none of them cost anything.

  1. 01 We read every registration Jessica and the front desk look at your dancer’s age and experience to find the class that actually fits.
  2. 02 We’ll call to set up a free trial class We pick the right class together, set a day and time for the trial, and answer anything you want to ask first.
  3. 03 Your dancer takes a trial class Come try it and let us know if they like it. If it feels like home, we’ll enroll them then. If not, no pressure and nothing owed.
